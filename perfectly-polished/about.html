Our story

Every pour tells a small, unrepeatable story.

Perfectly Polished began on a kitchen table, with a single mould and a lot of trial and error. What started as a way to preserve a handful of pressed flowers turned into a small studio devoted to one idea: resin, poured slowly and finished with care, can hold onto a moment better than almost anything else.

Today, every coaster, tray, wall piece and keepsake is still mixed, poured and cured by hand in small batches — never rushed, never mass-produced. Colours are layered gradually so the depth builds naturally, and every edge is sanded and buffed to a glass-like shine before it's ready to travel to its new home.

If you don't see the exact colours or size you're imagining in the gallery, reach out — most pieces can be made to order.

How each piece is made

From mould to mantelpiece

Design & mix

Colours, florals or pigments are chosen and mixed into food-safe, UV-resistant resin.

Pour & cure

Each layer is poured by hand and left to cure fully before the next goes on.

Sand & polish

Edges are sanded through several grits and buffed to a mirror-gloss finish.
